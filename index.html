What Joey does

Everything a serious reader needs.

📥
One-click saving

Save any article from any site instantly. Joey captures the content, source, thumbnail, and estimated reading time automatically.

🏷️
Tags, organisation & bulk actions

Tag articles as you save them and filter your library by tag. Select multiple articles at once to delete, archive, or move them in bulk. Star your favourites and keep your list exactly how you like it.

✏️
Highlights & notes

Select any passage in the reader and attach a personal note. Every highlight stays anchored to its exact location — always findable, always yours.

🔍
Full-text search

Search titles, sources, and exact phrases using quotes. Find anything in seconds, no matter how large your library grows.

🔊
Read Aloud

Listen to any saved article with built-in text-to-speech. Adjust playback speed and let Joey read while you commute, cook, or rest your eyes.

📤
Export & import

Export your entire library or a single article to Markdown or JSON — tags, highlights, and notes included. Import back anytime. Your reading history is yours, not locked in any silo.

💾

Back up your library regularly. Joey stores everything locally in your browser. Use Export all (JSON) or Export all (Markdown) frequently and save the file to your favourite cloud service — Google Drive, Dropbox, iCloud, or any folder you sync. This protects your reading history from accidental loss if you reset the browser or switch devices.
